Full itinerary — June 18–22, 2027
  • Fly to New York City and arrive at your hotel in Midtown Manhattan for check-in.
  • Pick-up rehearsal with your group.
  • Group welcome dinner — prepare for your time in New York City.
  • Breakfast at the hotel.
  • Meet your tour manager in the lobby.
  • Free time (will happen before or after rehearsal — based on rehearsal schedule).
  • Meet with the show and artistic director, then enjoy a 90-minute individual rehearsal session exclusively for your performing group.
  • Dinner at Ellen's Stardust Diner — the singing waitstaff will entertain you with popular show tunes throughout the meal.
  • Attend a one-woman stage show performed by the artistic director of the Arts for Autism Concert.
  • Breakfast at the hotel.
  • Morning rehearsal with a Broadway professional in preparation for the benefit concert. All groups will be together working with show directors and actors. Come prepared — wear performance shoes and rehearsal clothing. An Autism Speaks representative will also lead an orientation about autism awareness and performing for audiences that may include autistic children and adults.
  • Free time (will happen before or after rehearsal — based on rehearsal schedule).
  • Attend a Broadway show in the theater you will be performing in.
  • Dinner at Bubba Gump Shrimp Co.
  • Top of the Rock Experience — breathtaking panoramic views of the New York City Skyline.
  • Breakfast at the hotel.
  • Theatre orientation — hear the day's schedule, walk the stage, and review traffic patterns while technicians work on lighting and sound. Come prepared to run partial numbers wearing your performance shoes.
  • Lunch break (only performer meals included — due to quick turnaround for the performance).
  • Full technical rehearsal at the theatre with all student groups and Broadway cast members starting at 1PM. Full costumes required — no hair or makeup necessary. Students will observe Broadway performers, directors, choreographers, and lighting/sound designers collaborate in a real Broadway production. Come focused, patient, and flexible.
  • Pre-show dinner with all groups and cast members from your Broadway show. (Parents & Family/Fan included)
  • Perform in the evening Arts For Autism Concert — your group performs its own number alongside Broadway's biggest stars in an inspiring benefit performance. (Parents & Family/Fan get tickets to this event in lower orchestra)
  • Breakfast at the hotel.
  • Visit the 9/11 Memorial at the footprint of the former Twin Towers — a tribute of remembrance to the nearly 3,000 lives lost on September 11, 2001. The twin reflecting pools each cover nearly an acre and feature the largest manmade waterfalls in North America.
  • New York Harbor cruise with close-up views of the Statue of Liberty.
  • Visit Chinatown and Little Italy for free time to explore and shop.

Performer Package

+$550
The Performer Experience Fee covers the performance and educational components of the experience for active participants. Performers are required to attend all scheduled rehearsals, performances, workshops, and official activities while representing AXS Academy and ADX in a professional and positive manner.
  • Performance participation

Chaperone Package
Includes Chaperone Experience Fee
+$300
The Chaperone Experience Fee is designed for parents or guardians participating in an active support role during the experience. Chaperones may assist with performer supervision, lunch pickups, group coordination, transitions between activities, and supporting the overall experience environment.
  • Priority audience / event access
  • Chaperone credential / access designation
  • Participation in select scheduled group functions
  • Group communication access
  • On-site coordination support
  • Active involvement in light operational support
Family / Fan Package
Includes Family/Fan Experience Fee
+$150
The Family/Fan Experience Fee is designed for guests attending the experience with greater flexibility and without assigned support responsibilities. Family/Fan participants are not responsible for performer supervision, operational support, or required scheduled duties.
  • Priority audience / event access
  • Participation in included group activities
  • Flexible independent participation
  • Family/Fan credential / access designation
